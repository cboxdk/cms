# Fragments, the cache index and invalidation

Delivery answers are fragments keyed by their content keys; a change purges them with a fence at its commit position.

## The fragment store and the CDN driver

The fragment store and cache index (PRD 8.12 point 1, 9.3, 9.4) are the contract `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Cache\FragmentStore`: `write(Fragment): FragmentWriteOutcome` (`FragmentStored` or `FragmentFenced`), `read(FragmentKey): ?Fragment`, `fragmentsOf(DependencyKey): list<FragmentKey>` (the reverse index, live fragments, sorted) and `purge(FragmentPurge): list<FragmentKey>`. A `DependencyKey` is `e-<uuid>` or `n-<uuid>` (`DependencyKind`; `a-`, `q-` and `c-` come with their blocks), a `FragmentKey` 1 to 512 visible ASCII characters, and a `Fragment` holds its key, body, at most 50 dependency keys sorted and each once (PRD 9.6), the `CommitPosition` of the read it was built from (`builtAt`) and `validUntil`. Time is the Clock's: a fragment is gone once the Clock reaches its `validUntil`, a fence once it reaches its `fenceUntil`, and the stores keep their copies with that TTL. A purge writes the fence `purged:{key} = position` until `fenceUntil` and removes the key's fragments from the store and from the index of their other keys; while the fence lives, a fragment that depends on the key and was built at or below its position is refused as `FragmentFenced` and the key keeps what it held. A fence never moves down, and keeps the later end. A write or purge that has already ended throws `InvalidCacheValue`. The default is `Cbox\Cms\Core\Cache\Adapter\ValkeyFragmentStore`, bound in `cbox-cms.contracts`, on the default Redis connection, which must be phpredis to one Valkey primary: `write()` and `purge()` are one Lua script each (`cms:fragment:<key>` hashes, `cms:fragments_of:<dependency>` sets, `cms:purged:<dependency>` hashes), and positions are compared as decimal strings by length and bytes. The testkit has `FakeFragmentStore` and the shared suite `FragmentStoreContract`, run against the fake and, in the Contract suite with `RealValkey`, against Valkey (`packages/core/tests/Contract/ValkeyFragmentStoreContractTest.php`). The CDN driver is the contract `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Cdn\CdnDriver`: `purge(CdnPurge): CdnPurgeResult`, `supportsSoftPurge()` and `maxKeysPerRequest()`; a `CdnPurge` holds `DependencyKey`s as surrogate keys and a `PurgeMode` (`Soft`, `Hard`), a Soft purge on a driver without soft purge is applied Hard, a purge is split into requests of at most `maxKeysPerRequest()` keys, and a CDN that does not take a request throws `CdnUnavailable`. `cbox-cms.contracts` has no default for it, so resolving it throws `InvalidContractBinding` until an application configures one; the real drivers come with full-scale invalidation. The testkit has `FakeCdnDriver`, its own `CdnDriverHarness`, and the shared suite `CdnDriverContract`, run against a soft fake with three keys per request and a hard-only fake.

## The invalidation subscriber

The invalidation subscriber (PRD 7.6, 8.4, 8.12, 9.4) is `Cbox\Cms\Core\Fragments\Actions\InvalidateFragments` (`#[Internal]`), `#[Subscription('fragments.invalidate', events: [EntryCreated::class, VariantReleased::class, VariantRevised::class, VariantUnreleased::class, PlacementCreated::class, PlacementVisibilityChanged::class], lane: Lane::Critical, projection: 'origin')]`, so the receipt of every changeset that writes one of those events lists `origin` pending (`InvalidateFragments::PROJECTION`, the projection of `WaitLevel::Origin`). For each event it takes the content keys (`Fragments\Domain\ContentKeys::of()`, `e-{entry}` from the datum `entry` and, for a placement event, `n-{node}` from the datum `node`, the node the placement sits under, which `placement.visibility_changed` carries since the migration `*_add_node_to_placement_close.php`), purges each through `FragmentStore::purge()` with a fence at the event's commit position (its xid) until the Clock's now plus `cbox-cms.fragments.fence_seconds` (default 60, 1 to 86400, `Fragments\Boundary\InvalidationConfig` into `Domain\Dto\InvalidationSettings`), then purges them in one `CdnPurge` through the `CdnDriver`, soft for a change and hard for a removal (`Fragments\Domain\EdgePurge`: `variant.unreleased` and a `placement.visibility_changed` to anything but live), then `markProjection()`s `origin` acknowledged at the Clock's now in the runner's batch; a `CdnUnavailable` rolls the batch back, so origin waits for both purges. The critical lane therefore needs a `CdnDriver` in `cbox-cms.contracts`. The workbench sets it to the testkit's `FakeCdnDriver` when its environment has `CBOX_CMS_CDN_DRIVER=fake`, and the runner's service actor from `CBOX_CMS_EVENTS_SERVICE_ACTOR` (`WorkbenchServiceProvider::configureEventRunner()`), so a `vendor/bin/testbench cms:events:run` process runs it. The tests are `packages/core/tests/Actions/InvalidateFragmentsTest.php` (fakes, `Tests\Fragments\InvalidationWorld`), `packages/core/tests/Postgres/FragmentInvalidationTest.php` (a revise's receipt lists origin pending, acknowledged after the container's runner handled it) `tests/Postgres/WalkingSkeleton/InvalidateOnPublishingTest.php` (a release, an unpublish, and a placement and publish at a path that answered 404, each at origin with a runner process) and `tests/Postgres/WalkingSkeleton/RestartAfterCommitTest.php` (a runner process killed before it handled a committed revise, and a new process that invalidates the fragment); `EntryWorld` takes another time than `NOW` for a world that shares receipts with a process on the system clock.
