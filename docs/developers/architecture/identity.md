# Identity, login and sessions

Identity is split between the core (the actor aggregate and its commands) and the identity module (login, local accounts and sessions). Both are here.

## The identity contracts

Identity (PRD 5.16, 2.31, 12.2) is two contracts in `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Identity`. `ActorDirectory::find(ActorId): ?Actor` reads an actor as an aggregate: `ActorId` (in `Contracts\Ids`, a UUIDv7), `ActorClass` (`staff`, `end_user`, `service`), `ActorState` (`pending`, `active`, `deactivated`, `deprovisioned`), `version` and `CredentialGeneration`, both from 1. `CredentialVerifier::verify(?TransportCredential): Principal` gives `AnonymousPrincipal` (ceiling `public`, invariant 25) for no credential, or `ActorPrincipal` (actor, on-behalf-of chain in order, `IssuerKind` `human` for a session, `agent` or `service`, classification ceiling), and otherwise throws `CredentialRejected` with the first `CredentialErrorCode` that applies: `credential_malformed` (form or CRC-32 checksum of `ServiceCredentialToken`, found without a lookup), `credential_unknown`, `credential_expired`, `actor_not_active` (the actor or anyone in the chain) and `credential_revoked` (generation below the actor's). Every verifier decides through `IssuedCredential::principal()`. `ClassificationAccess` orders `public` to `sensitive`; `IssuerKind::maximumCeiling()` is `confidential` for an agent (PRD 2.31), and `ActorPrincipal`, `IssuedCredential` and the testkit's `ServiceCredentialSpec` throw `InvalidIdentity` above it. `AccessContext` (principal, disjoint `AccessRegion`s of `NodePath`s with their exceptions, where a region lies inside another only within one of its exceptions, classification access at most the principal's ceiling; `AccessContext::anonymous()`) is what the pipelines consume. The defaults are `Cbox\Cms\Core\Identity\Adapter\PostgresActorDirectory` and `PostgresCredentialVerifier`, which read on the default connection's write PDO. The core migration `*_create_identity_tables.php` makes `actors`, `service_credentials` (the SHA-256 of the token, unique, the generation at issue, the issuer kind, the ceiling with a CHECK that keeps an agent at `confidential`, and an expiry) and `service_credential_delegations` (the chain), each with forced row level security, a write policy for the owner role alone, and SELECT alone for the app role, so the app role reads no row of them without an actor context; the directory and the verifier run before a context exists and read one row by its key through the access migration's lookup functions `cms_identity_actor`, `cms_identity_credential` and `cms_identity_delegations` (SECURITY DEFINER as the owner role, with a fixed search_path), never a list. Until the identity block brings login and the issuing commands, actors and credentials are written only by the testkit's seeders (`IdentitySeeder`): `FakeIdentity`, the fake of both contracts, and `Cbox\Cms\Testkit\FixtureWriters\Identity\Adapter\PostgresIdentitySeeder`, a fixture writer that writes as the owner role. The shared suites `ActorDirectoryContract` and `CredentialVerifierContract` take an `IdentityHarness` (a seeder with `directory()` and `verifier()`); the Postgres runs are `packages/core/tests/Contract/Postgres*ContractTest.php` in the Contract suite, with the harness `Cbox\Cms\Core\Tests\Identity\PostgresIdentity`.

## The identity module and its credential store

Identity is split in two (PRD 5.16). `Cbox\Cms\Core\Identity` is the actor aggregate and the actor commands: `actors`, the `ActorDirectory` and `CredentialVerifier` defaults, and `actor.deactivate`. `Cbox\Cms\Identity` (`packages/identity`) is login: the local accounts, their credential store and, as B1 brings them, the login pages and sessions. It builds on the core and the contracts and never uses the testkit or the generators; core, http, cli and mcp never use it (`FORBIDDEN_MODULES`). The credential store is isolated by privilege (PRD 5.16, "Lokale konti"): its tables live in the schema `cms_identity` (`Identity\CredentialStore\Domain\CredentialStore`), which the owner role owns and creates in `docker/postgres/sql/database.sql` and the testkit's `TestDatabaseSetup` (`IDENTITY_SCHEMA`), with CONNECT, USAGE and, through the owner's default privileges there, DML on its tables for the identity role `cms_identity` of `docker/postgres/sql/roles.sql` alone, and nothing for the app role or PUBLIC; so the tables have no row level security. The identity role connects on the connection `cbox-cms.identity.connection` names, the workbench's `pgsql_identity` (`DB_IDENTITY_USERNAME` and `DB_IDENTITY_PASSWORD` in `phpunit.xml`, `CMS_IDENTITY_ROLE` and `CMS_IDENTITY_PASSWORD` in `compose.yaml`, `compose.ci.yaml` and `ci.yml`, held equal by `tests/Feature/Tooling/Ci`), with the schema as its search path. The owner connection's search path lists `cms_identity` after the kernel's schema, so `migrate:fresh` rebuilds its tables and the harness truncates them after each test; the Postgres harness checks that and grants the identity connection's role the schema in each checkout's and worker's database, which `CheckoutConnections` points it at. The module's migrations, in `packages/identity/database/migrations` and run as the owner role, create `cms_identity.local_accounts` (the actor as primary key referencing `actors`, the lowercased unique login, the Argon2id password hash, `password_changed_at`, `version`, `created_at`, a CHECK on every form) and `cms_identity.password_reset_tokens` (the SHA-256 of the token as key, `actor_id` with its own index, `expires_at`, `used_at`, `created_at`). They are not kernel tables: `KernelTablesTest` reads only the kernel's schema, and `KernelTableWriteRule` reports a write to a `cms_identity` table, or to an unqualified table of `CredentialTables::TABLES`, outside `Cbox\Cms\Identity` and `packages/identity`'s migrations. `IdentityServiceProvider` adds four blocking checks to `cbox-cms.doctor.checks`, the fourth `identity.session_cookie` of the sessions below: `identity.connection` (the connection answers, and its user is neither the app role nor the owner role), `identity.credential_isolation` (the schema exists, the app role holds no privilege on it or its relations, and the identity role is no superuser, has no BYPASSRLS or CREATEROLE and no privileged membership, as `RoleMembership` defines it) and `identity.argon2id`, on the probes `Identity\Doctor\Domain\Probes\CredentialStoreProbe` and `PasswordHashingProbe`, with fakes in `packages/identity/tests/Doctor/Fakes`. `docs/security/credential-store.md` tells an operator how to create the role, the schema and the connection in production.

## The login policy

The login policy (PRD 5.16, invariant 38) is the identity module's feature `Cbox\Cms\Identity\LoginPolicy`: `cbox-cms.identity.policy` per actor class (`staff`, `end_user`; a service actor never logs in) with `connections` and `methods` as maps from a name to a bool (so an application's file merges key by key), `local_login`, `local_factors` (`password` or `passkey_or_two_factors`, the default for staff; the workbench sets `password`), `federated_amr`, `federated_acr`, `inactivity_minutes` and `absolute_minutes`, and `authoritative_connections`, read by `Boundary\LoginPolicyConfig` into the singleton `Domain\Dto\LoginPolicy` (`InvalidLoginPolicy`, `login_policy_invalid`). A session is issued only with a `Domain\LoginDecision`, whose constructor is private, so only its `decide()` makes one (`packages/identity/tests/Phpstan` analyses a fixture that calls `new LoginDecision` and expects `new.privateConstructor`). Login paths call the action `Actions\CheckLoginPolicy::check(LoginAttempt)`, which reads the actor through the `ActorDirectory`, decides in the order of `docs/security/login-policy.md` (actor_not_active, login_class_not_allowed, login_connection_not_allowed, login_method_not_allowed, login_local_disabled, login_authoritative_link, login_factors_unavailable; `LoginPolicyRefused` with a `LoginPolicyErrorCode`) and counts `cms.login.decisions` by `cms.outcome` and `cms.error.code`. The local connection is `LoginPolicy::LOCAL_CONNECTION` (`local`). The IdP links are the port `Domain\IdpLinks`, bound to `Adapter\PostgresIdpLinks` on the identity connection over `cms_identity.idp_links` (migration `*_create_idp_links_table.php`; connection, issuer and subject as the key, the actor); its fake is `packages/identity/tests/LoginPolicy/Fakes/FakeIdpLinks`, held to it by `IdpLinksBehaviour`. The tests are `packages/identity/tests/Unit/LoginPolicyTest.php` (`LoginWorld`).

## Local accounts

Local accounts (PRD 5.16, "Lokale konti") are the contract `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Identity\LocalCredentialStore` (`#[Experimental]`): `bind(ActorId, LoginIdentifier, PasswordHash)` (`LocalAccountExists`, `local_account_exists`, for a login or an actor bound already; `InvalidIdentity` for an actor the register does not have), `find()`, `ofActor()`, `rehash(actor, verified, rehashed)` (only while the account has the hash verified; keeps `passwordChangedAt`), `changePassword()` (`LocalAccountMissing`, `local_account_missing`), `issueResetToken(actor, expiresAt)` and `resetPassword(PasswordResetToken, PasswordHash)` (once, before expiry; `PasswordResetRefused`, `password_reset_token_invalid`, for unknown, used and expired alike); every hash change adds 1 to `LocalAccount::$version`. It stores hashes only: `LoginIdentifier` is the email in lower case (`fromEmail()`, `typed()`), `PasswordHash` an Argon2id hash in PHP's encoded form, `PasswordResetToken` `cms_pr_` with 32 random bytes and a CRC-32 (kept as its SHA-256). `cbox-cms.contracts` binds it to the identity module's `CredentialStore\Adapter\PostgresLocalCredentialStore` on `cbox-cms.identity.connection`; the testkit has `Identity\FakeLocalCredentialStore` (its own harness, actors from an `ActorDirectory`, by default a `FakeIdentity`) and the shared suite `LocalCredentialStoreContract` (`LocalCredentialStoreHarness`), run against the fake in `packages/testkit/tests/Contract` and against Postgres in `packages/identity/tests/Contract` (`Tests\LocalAccounts\PostgresLocalAccounts`). The feature `Cbox\Cms\Identity\LocalAccounts` has `Domain\PasswordPolicy` (at most 1024 bytes, at least 12 characters, then `BreachedPasswords`; `PasswordRefused` with a `PasswordErrorCode`: `password_too_long`, `password_too_short`, `password_breached`), the port `Domain\PasswordHasher` (bound to `Adapter\Argon2idPasswordHasher` at `cbox-cms.identity.passwords.argon2id`, `Domain\Dto\Argon2idParameters`, one thread; `dummy()` is a fixed hash at those parameters that no password verifies against) and `Domain\LocalConnection`, the `LoginConnection` `local` of the flow Direct: it verifies the password against the account's hash once, and an unknown or malformed login or an empty password once against the dummy hash, refuses a password over 1024 bytes before hashing, rehashes a hash of other parameters, and gives a `VerifiedAssertion` with the issuer `cbox-cms.identity.local.issuer` (null takes `app.url`, `Boundary\LocalAccountsConfig`), the actor's id as subject, the Clock's time and amr `pwd`; every refusal is `login_rejected`. `Cbox\Cms\Identity\Staff\Actions\RegisterLocalStaff::register(StaffRegistration)` refuses a login with an account and a password the policy refuses before anything is written, hashes, then runs `actor.register` (staff, pending), binds the credential and runs `actor.activate`, both through the core's `RunMaintenanceCommand` with the unit of work `staff:<actor id>`, as an operation through the `OperationRunner` (`Staff\Actions\StaffRegistrationChunks`, kind `identity.staff_registration`, keyed by the SHA-256 of the login, chunks `register:`, `bind:` and `activate:<actor id>`); a failure after the first step leaves the actor pending (`StaffRegistrationRefused::$pending`) and the operation running, and a rerun for the login resumes it with the same actor; a rerun whose login's account belongs to a pending staff actor (read through the `ActorDirectory`) sets the rerun's password with `changePassword()`, and when no registration of that actor is running it runs `actor.activate` at the version read under `staff:<that actor id>`. `cms:staff:create --email --name [--password-stdin]` is the identity module's `Cli\Console\StaffCreateCommand` (the cli module may not use identity), with `Staff\Boundary\StaffCreateInput` and `StaffCreateOutput` (the cli's `CliAnswer`): the password hidden from the terminal twice or from standard input, never an argument; it prints the actor's id. The tests: `packages/identity/tests/Actions/RegisterLocalStaffTest.php` (`Tests\Staff\StaffWorld`), `Unit/PasswordPolicyTest.php`, `Unit/LocalConnectionTimingTest.php` (`Tests\LocalAccounts\CountingPasswordHasher`), `Contract/LocalLoginConnectionContractTest.php` and its Postgres twin, `Postgres/StaffSecretsTest.php` (no email or password in the log, FakeTelemetry or messages) and `packages/cli/tests/Postgres/StaffCreateCommandTest.php`. The pages are `docs/addons/contracts/local-credential-store.md` and `docs/security/local-accounts.md`.

## Sessions

Sessions (PRD 5.16, "Sessioner og credentials") are the identity module's feature `Cbox\Cms\Identity\Sessions`, and a session is a credential. Its id is the contracts' `Identity\SessionToken` (`cms_ss_`, 32 random bytes as hex, a CRC-32 checksum), carried as the session form of a `TransportCredential` (`TransportCredential::session()`, `CredentialForm::Session`; every verifier reads each form only as itself), and verifies through `IssuedSession::principal()` to an `ActorPrincipal` of `IssuerKind::Human`, whose ceiling is sensitive. `IssuerKind::envelopeIssuer()` maps human, agent and service to the envelope's human, agent and system, which `RunExposedCommand` and the `QueryPipeline` (`QueryResult::$issuer`) use. The action `Actions\IssueSession::issue(LoginDecision)` always makes a new id and stores the session through the port `Domain\SessionStore` under its SHA-256 (`Domain\SessionKey`) with the actor, class, connection, method, the factors the login gave (`LoginPolicy\Domain\LocalFactors`, from the decision), IdP `sid` (`Domain\IdpSessionId`, from `LoginAttempt` through the decision) or null, generation and the issued and last-seen times (`Domain\Dto\StoredSession`); `Actions\EndSessions` ends one at logout, every session of an actor, or every session of a (connection, sid), through the store's sets, never a scan. `Domain\SessionRules` decides expiry (the earlier of last seen plus `inactivity_minutes` and issued plus `absolute_minutes` of the policy now), the state and generation, and whether the policy still allows the connection and method and, for a local session, local login, the factors it gave (`LocalFactors::satisfies()`) and no link of the actor to an authoritative connection, read through `IdpLinks` (invariant 38) (`credential_not_allowed`, `CredentialErrorCode::NotAllowed`); the store keeps a session `SessionRules::KEPT_AFTER_END_MINUTES` past its end so a late request is told `credential_expired`. The store is `Adapter\ValkeySessionStore` on the default Redis connection (phpredis, one primary; one Lua script per change; keys `cms:session:`, `cms:sessions_of_actor:`, `cms:sessions_of_idp:`), with the fake `packages/identity/tests/Sessions/Fakes/FakeSessionStore` held to it by `SessionStoreBehaviour` (`packages/identity/tests/Postgres/ValkeySessionStoreBehaviourTest.php` with `RealValkey`). `IdentityServiceProvider` decorates the bound `CredentialVerifier` with `Adapter\SessionCredentialVerifier` through the container's `extend()`, so the core never names the module: it verifies the session form, reads the actor through the `ActorDirectory` in Postgres (no Valkey mirror yet), ends a refused session and renews a valid one, and hands every other credential on. The counters are `cms.session.issued` (`cms.login.method`) and `cms.session.ended` (`cms.reason`, `Domain\SessionEndReason`). The cookie is `Domain\Dto\SessionCookie` from `cbox-cms.identity.session.cookie` per environment (`Boundary\SessionCookieConfig`; an environment without an entry takes `production`'s: `__Host-cms_session`, Secure, SameSite Lax; local and testing `cms_session` without Secure; always HttpOnly, path /, no Domain), bound in the container; the provider refuses to boot a process that serves HTTP (`ProcessWorkload`) with an invalid cookie (`InvalidSessionCookie`, `session_cookie_invalid`) or, outside local and testing, one that is not Secure, lacks `__Host-` or is SameSite=None (`InsecureSessionCookie`, `session_cookie_insecure`), and the blocking doctor check `identity.session_cookie` (`SessionCookieCheck` on the probe `SessionCookieProbe`) says why. The testkit's `FakeIdentity` holds sessions too (`startSession()`), and `SessionCredentialContract` with `SessionIdentityHarness` is the shared suite of a verifier that holds sessions, run against the fake and the decorator. The tests are `packages/identity/tests/Unit/SessionLifetimeTest.php` (`SessionWorld`), `SessionCookieTest.php` and `packages/identity/tests/Postgres/SessionCredentialTest.php`; `docs/security/sessions.md` documents it.

## The login contracts

Login (PRD 5.16, "Identitetskontrakten" and "Koblinger") is two contracts in `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Identity\Login`, `#[Experimental]` until the identity module's connections and OpenID Connect implementation (B1 part 2) have tested them; neither has a default in `cbox-cms.contracts`. `LoginConnection` (`id()`, `flow()`, `start(): LoginStarted` and `complete(PendingLogin, LoginResponse): VerifiedAssertion`) serves both a direct credential check (`LoginFlow::Direct`, `SubmittedCredentials`) and a redirect flow (`LoginFlow::Redirect`, `LoginStarted::$redirectTo`, `CallbackParameters`); the caller keeps the `PendingLogin` (connection, flow, `LoginState` of at least 128 random bits, the connection's secrets) server-side and removes it before `complete()`, which first runs `PendingLogin::check()` (`login_state_mismatch`, constant time), then the credentials or token (`login_rejected`). `VerifiedAssertion` holds the connection, `Issuer`, `Subject` (`identity()`: an `IdpIdentity`), `authTime` in UTC, `amr` (`AuthenticationMethod`s, each once, empty when none is sent), `acr` or null and `groups` (`IdpGroup`s, each once) or null when the connection sends none; a duplicate or a value out of form is `InvalidIdentity`. `IssuerResolver` (`pin(ConnectionId): IssuerPin`, `admit(ConnectionId, TokenClaims): IdpIdentity`, `UnknownConnection` for a connection without a pin) decides through `IssuerPin::admit()`: another issuer is `login_issuer_mismatch`, then for a `TenantPin` (a `TenantClaim` such as `tid` or `hd` and a `TenantId`) a token without the claim, or with it empty, is `login_tenant_claim_missing` and another tenant `login_tenant_mismatch`; the tenant is read only from the verified token's `TokenClaims`, never from the request. Refusals are `LoginRefused` with a `LoginErrorCode`. The testkit has `Login\FakeLoginConnection` (its own harness, `enrol()` of `FakeLoginAccount`s, `respondAs()`), `Login\FakeIssuerResolver` and the shared suites `LoginConnectionContract` (`LoginConnectionHarness`, `AcceptedLogin`) and `IssuerResolverContract` (`IssuerResolverHarness`), run in `packages/testkit/tests/Contract` against both flows; `packages/testkit/tests/Login/PlantedResolverTest.php` holds the resolver suite to its purpose with planted resolvers. The pages are `docs/addons/contracts/login-connection.md` and `issuer-resolver.md`.

## Lifecycle signals and provisioning

Lifecycle signals and provisioning (PRD 5.16, "Signaler fra IdP'en") are three contracts, `#[Experimental]` and without a default in `cbox-cms.contracts` until the identity module's implementations (B1 part 2 and B6). `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Identity\Signals\BackChannelLogoutReceiver::receive(ConnectionId, LogoutToken): LogoutOutcome` takes the verified claims of an OpenID Connect back-channel logout token (`iss`, `aud` as `Audience`s, `iat`, `exp`, `jti` as a `SignalId`, `events` as `EventTypeUri`s, `sub`, `sid` as an `IdpSessionId`, and whether a `nonce` was there) and gives the sessions that end, of the IdP session (`LogoutScope::IdpSession`) or of the subject (`LogoutScope::Subject`); a replayed `jti` of the same issuer is `signal_replayed`. `SecurityEventReceiver::receive(ConnectionId, SecurityEventToken): SecurityEventOutcome` takes an SSF security event of one type: `SecurityEventKind` (CAEP session-revoked and credential-change, RISC account-disabled, account-enabled, account-purged and credential-compromise) and its `SignalAction` (end sessions, or actor.deactivate, reactivate, deprovision or credentials_revoke); the subject must be a `SubjectIdentifier` of format `iss_sub` of the pinned issuer (RFC 9493), never an email alone; the first admitted `jti` is `SecurityEventApplied`, later ones `SecurityEventReplayed`. Both decide through `SignalPin` (connection, issuer, audience) `admitLogout()` and `admitEvent()` at the Clock's time with `CLOCK_SKEW_SECONDS` (60), refuse with `SignalRefused` and a `SignalErrorCode` (`signal_*`, 400), remember a `jti` per issuer only once admitted, and throw `Login\UnknownConnection` for a connection without a pin. `Identity\Provisioning\ScimProvisioning` is SCIM 2.0 for the users (`ScimUser`: `externalId` as a `Subject`, the immutable value of the connection's declared claim, `ScimUserName`, display name, email, `active`) and groups (`ScimGroup`, members each once, `MembershipChange`) of the connection whose token made the call: create, replace, `setUserActive()`, delete, read, and group membership; another connection's resource is `scim_resource_not_found` and its user as a member `scim_invalid_value`. An outcome (`ScimUserOutcome`, `ScimGroupOutcome`, `ScimDeletion`) holds the `ScimChange`s and a `ScimIdempotencyKey` of connection, resource, desired state and the `ResourceVersion` before the change, whose `unitOfWork()` the command's envelope takes; a call that asks for the state the resource has changes nothing and has no key. Refusals are `ScimRefused` with a `ScimErrorCode` (`scim_*`, with `scimType()`; `scim_version_mismatch` is 412, `HttpStatus::PreconditionFailed`); only the connection that deactivated an actor reactivates it (`scim_reactivation_refused`). SCIM token issuance is not in the contract. The testkit has `Signals\FakeBackChannelLogoutReceiver`, `Signals\FakeSecurityEventReceiver` and `Provisioning\FakeScimProvisioning`, each its own harness, and the shared suites `BackChannelLogoutContract` (`BackChannelLogoutHarness`), `SecurityEventReceiverContract` (`SecurityEventHarness`) and `ScimProvisioningContract` (`ScimProvisioningHarness`, with `deactivateElsewhere()`), run in `packages/testkit/tests/Contract`; `packages/testkit/tests/Signals/PlantedReceiversTest.php` holds each suite to its purpose with planted implementations. The pages are `docs/addons/contracts/back-channel-logout.md`, `security-event-receiver.md` and `scim-provisioning.md`.

## Breached passwords

Breached passwords (PRD 5.16) are the contract `Cbox\Cms\Contracts\Identity\BreachedPasswords` (`#[Experimental]`): `isBreached(Password): bool`, which throws the retryable `BreachedPasswordsUnavailable` (`breached_passwords_unavailable`) when it cannot tell and never answers false for an unchecked password. `Contracts\Identity\Password` holds the typed password in a closure, read only by `reveal()`; its string form, JSON, dumps and export show `Password::REDACTED`, `serialize()` throws, and its constructor's parameter is `#[SensitiveParameter]`. The default, which `IdentityServiceProvider` puts in `cbox-cms.contracts` when the application names none, is the identity module's `Cbox\Cms\Identity\BreachedPasswords\Adapter\HibpBreachedPasswords`: the range API of Pwned Passwords through the egress gateway (host class `breached_passwords`), with only the first five hex digits of the SHA-1 in the URL and `Add-Padding: true`, padding lines (count 0) never a match, and the counter `cms.identity.breached_passwords.checks` under `cms.outcome` (`breached`, `clean`, `unavailable`). The testkit has `Identity\FakeBreachedPasswords` and the shared suite `Identity\BreachedPasswordsContract` with its `BreachedPasswordsHarness`, run against the fake and, with the range API served behind the fake gateway (`packages/identity/tests/BreachedPasswords/HibpRangeService`), against the adapter (`vendor/bin/pest --testsuite=Contract --filter=BreachedPasswords`); `packages/identity/tests/Unit/HibpRangeRequestTest.php` holds what it sends. `docs/addons/contracts/breached-passwords.md` documents it.
