# The kernel's commands and queries

Each command is a feature of the core module with its command DTO, its action, its reader port, its mutation writers and version locks, and its tests. A new command follows `docs/recipes/kernel-action.md` and adds its paragraph here.

## The entry commands

The entry commands (PRD 4.1, 5.4, 6.4, 11.6) live in the core module `Cbox\Cms\Core\Entries`: the commands `Domain\Commands\CreateEntry` (`entry.create` version 1: the caller's `EntryId`, the `TypeId`, the home `NodeId` and the `FieldValues` of the shared variant; it expects the entry absent) and `Domain\Commands\ReviseEntry` (`entry.revise` version 1: the entry, the `AggregateVersion` of its shared variant the caller read, and every field), both `ExpectsVersions`, and their actions `Actions\CreateEntryAction` and `Actions\ReviseEntryAction`, `#[Action]` on the surfaces rest, inertia, mcp and cli. resolve() reads through the port `Domain\EntryReader` (bound to `Adapter\PostgresEntryReader`, one lookup by key under the actor context, with a fake and `EntryReaderBehaviour` in `packages/core/tests/Entries`); a create reads the entry and its variant as absent and the home node at its version, a revise the entry and the variant. A create plans `EntryCreated`, `RevisionCreated` 1 and `HeadMoved` null to 1; a revise `RevisionCreated` and `HeadMoved` from the head's revision to the next, for the entry's own type. The core tags the version locks `Entries\Adapter\PostgresEntryVersionLock` (`entry`), `PostgresVariantVersionLock` (`variant`, the row of `variant_heads`) and `Structure\Adapter\PostgresNodeVersionLock` (`node`), FOR SHARE or FOR NO KEY UPDATE, and the writers `EntryCreatedWriter` (the `entries` row; `entry.created`), `RevisionCreatedWriter` (the `variant_heads` row with the first revision; for `history: full` the `revisions` row, a draft, and its `revision_payloads` row; for audit-only or none the `head_snapshots` row with its `rev_no`, the column the migration `*_add_revision_number_to_head_snapshots.php` adds; then the type table's row through `Adapter\TypeRows`: the draft row for `stages: draft-release`, removed again when it equals the released row, and the released row for `stages: none`) and `HeadMovedWriter` (the head's draft revision, schema version and version in one statement; `variant.revised`). The events are `Domain\Events\EntryCreated` and `Domain\Events\VariantRevised` with the payloads `EntryCreatedV1` and `VariantRevisedV1`; `EntryId`, `NodeId`, `TypeId` and `VariantRef` implement `Identifier` for them. `Entries\Boundary\StoredContent` gives the payload (the `FieldValuesInput` form as JSON, format 1) and the type row's column values. The pipeline refuses an entry whose home node its action read as absent with `validation_failed`, and a value for an encrypted field with `field_encryption_unavailable` until key management comes (PRD 12.3, B6). The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/CreateEntryTest.php` and `ReviseEntryTest.php` (`EntryActionWorld`), `packages/core/tests/Postgres/EntryCommandsTest.php` (`EntryWorld`, the workbench's `fixture_article` and `fixture_measurement`), `tests/Postgres/WalkingSkeleton/ConcurrentSavesTest.php` (two revises, and two creates, on `IndependentConnections`, interleaved after the first's resolve) and `IdempotentRepeatsTest.php`. `docs/addons/entry-commands.md` documents them.

## The placement commands

The placement commands (PRD 4.1, 5.7, 5.9, 5.10, 6.4, invariants 14, 15 and 18) live in the core module `Cbox\Cms\Core\Placements`: `Domain\Commands\CreatePlacement` (`placement.create` version 1: the caller's `PlacementId`, the `EntryId`, the `NodeId`, the `SiteId` and a list of `Domain\Dto\LocaleSlug`, a `Locale` and a `Contracts\Content\Slug` each; it expects the placement absent) and `Domain\Commands\SetPlacementWindow` (`placement.set_window` version 1: the placement, the `AggregateVersion` the caller read, the `Locale` and a `?TimeWindow`, null to hide), with `Actions\CreatePlacementAction` and `Actions\SetPlacementWindowAction`, `#[Action]` on rest, inertia, mcp and cli, whose plans come from `Actions\PlacementPlanner` (for commands that compose them, such as entry.publish). They read through the port `Domain\PlacementReader` (bound to `Adapter\PostgresPlacementReader`, a fake and `PlacementReaderBehaviour` in `packages/core/tests/Placements`) at the Clock's time: the placement below a node the actor reaches (`cms_access_node`), its version, the node, the site (readable by every context through `sites_read` and `site_locales_read`), whether each slug is taken, and every placement of the entry in the locale past the actor's regions through the owner function `cms_placement_locales`. Both implement the contract `Pipeline\RefusesCommand`, which the `CommandPipeline` asks after the authorize phase: a node or a placement's node the actor does not reach is `unauthorized` (placement rights are decided on the placement's node, PRD 5.10), a slug another placement that is not withdrawn has below the node is `placement_slug_taken`, and a node outside the site, a mount, a locale the site does not publish in, an entry the actor cannot read or a withdrawn placement is `validation_failed`. A window that would store the placement live or scheduled is `validation_failed` at `window` unless the entry is active and its shared variant has a released revision, or its type has stages none and the variant is not withdrawn (invariant 6; `Domain\Dto\EntryRelease::showable()`): `PlacementReader::entryRelease()` reads that past the actor's regions through the owner function `cms_entry_release`, and the aggregate `Domain\EntryReleaseRef` (`entry_release`, the shared head's version while the entry is active, locked by `Adapter\PostgresEntryReleaseLock` through `cms_entry_release_lock`) makes a release taken back meanwhile `version_conflict`; the migration `*_add_entry_release_reads.php` adds both functions and the owner role's read and lock policies on `entries` and `variant_heads`. The pipeline rejects a plan with a `Plans\ChangesPublicVisibility` mutation that `makesPublic()`, such as `PlacementWindowSet` with a window, from an agent issuer or an agent credential with `agent_visibility_forbidden` (invariant 18); it rejects the same way an agent's revision of a type with stages none while a placement of its entry is visible now or later, read past the actor's regions through the port `Pipeline\Domain\PublicPlacements` (bound to `Placements\Adapter\ReaderPublicPlacements`, `PlacementReader::everyLocale()` at the Clock's time; `FakePublicPlacements` held to it by `PublicPlacementsBehaviour`), and otherwise adds every placement of the entry to the call's reads, so a window opened meanwhile is `version_conflict` (security review S-4; tests `packages/core/tests/Actions/AgentUnstagedSaveTest.php` and `packages/core/tests/Postgres/AgentUnstagedSaveTest.php`). `Domain\CanonicalRule` keeps one canonical placement per entry and locale, a visible one whenever one is visible, and the plans move the flag with `PlacementCanonicalSet` (clear before set); the aggregates `Domain\PlacementSlugRef` (`placement_slug`) and `Domain\CanonicalPlacementRef` (`placement_canonical`), read as absent behind the commit's advisory lock, make two commands that claim one slug or would each make a placement canonical commit one after the other. The core tags the version locks `Adapter\PostgresPlacementVersionLock` (`placement`, through `cms_placement_lock`), `PostgresPlacementSlugLock`, `PostgresCanonicalPlacementLock` and `Structure\Adapter\PostgresSiteVersionLock` (`site`, through `cms_structure_lock_site`), and the writers `PlacementCreatedWriter` (`placements` and the released `placement_generations` row; `placement.created`), `PlacementLocaleAddedWriter` (the released `placement_locales` row, hidden), `PlacementWindowSetWriter` (window, `Domain\Visibility` at the changeset's time and `next_transition_at`; `placement.visibility_changed`) and `PlacementCanonicalSetWriter` (through the owner function `cms_placement_set_canonical`, which runs only in the transaction of a changeset by the context's actor, because the placement that loses the flag may lie outside the actor's regions); migration `*_add_placement_commands.php`. M1 writes the released stage only and has no scheduler, so a read decides visibility from the window at its own time (block B3 moves the stored state). The structure comes from the testkit's `FixtureWriters\Structure\Adapter\PostgresStructureFixtures` (sites with their locales and root route, nodes, mounts and routes, as the owner role through the `<table>_owner_write` policies on `nodes`, `sites`, `site_locales` and `node_routes`). The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/CreatePlacementTest.php` and `SetPlacementWindowTest.php` (`PlacementActionWorld`), and `packages/core/tests/Postgres/PlacementCommandsTest.php` (`PlacementWorld`: one entry on two sites with one canonical placement, the flag moved across regions, refusals, a slug race on `IndependentConnections`, and the same queries with 20 and 200 placements below the node). `docs/addons/placement-commands.md` documents them.

## The release command

The release command (PRD 4.1, 5.6, 6.4, invariants 5 and 18) lives in `Cbox\Cms\Core\Entries` too: `Domain\Commands\ReleaseVariant` (`variant.release` version 1: the entry, the `RevisionNumber` to release and the `AggregateVersion` of the shared variant the caller read; `ExpectsVersions`) and `Actions\ReleaseVariantAction`, `#[Action]` on rest, inertia, mcp and cli, which reads through `EntryReader` and plans with `Actions\VariantReleasePlanner`, the planner a composite command composes: `VariantReleased` (entry, type, variant, revision; the contract mutation names the type) for the entry's own type, or an empty plan when the revision is the released one already. `StoredHead` holds the draft's number, the variant's highest number (`latest`, which the next revise follows, because a release writes a revision after the draft) and the released revision's number (`released`, null unless the release state is released). `VariantReleased` implements `ChangesPublicVisibility` and always `makesPublic()`, so the pipeline refuses a release with `agent_visibility_forbidden` when the credential's `Identity\IssuerKind` or the envelope's issuer kind is agent (invariant 18), as it refuses a placement's window; it refuses a release of a type with `stages: none` or a history other than full with `type_not_releasable`; and, in phase 5, reads the released revision through the port `Pipeline\Domain\RevisionContents` (bound to `Entries\Adapter\PostgresRevisionContents`, one lookup by key, the payload read back with `StoredContent::fields()`; a fake and `RevisionContentsBehaviour` in `packages/core/tests/Pipeline`) and validates it at `ValidationStage::Release` below the path `revision`, or rejects with `validation_failed` a revision the variant does not have or one written under another schema version than the type's. The writer `Adapter\VariantReleasedWriter` keeps a released draft as a new revision of kind `published` after the variant's highest number with a copy of the payload (a published revision is released as it is), points the head at it with release state released in one statement (a withdrawn head throws; only reinstate releases it), inserts the `release_log` row and writes the released row through `TypeRows::release()`, which first keeps the old released row as the draft row when there is none, then writes the released row and removes the draft row when it is equal; it returns `variant.released` (`Domain\Events\VariantReleased`, `VariantReleasedV1`: revision, published, previous). The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/ReleaseVariantTest.php` (`EntryActionWorld`), `packages/core/tests/Postgres/VariantReleaseTest.php` (`EntryWorld`, with the query count at 20 and 200 revisions), and `docs/addons/release-command.md` documents it.

## The publish commands

The publish commands (PRD 5.6, 5.10, 6.2, 6.4, invariants 7, 14 and 18) live in the core module `Cbox\Cms\Core\Publishing`: `Domain\Commands\PublishEntry` (`entry.publish` version 1: the entry, the shared variant's `AggregateVersion`, a `?RevisionNumber` to release, null for and only for a type with `stages: none`, the home `PlacementId` with its `AggregateVersion`, the `Locale` and a `?TimeWindow`, null for live from now) and `Domain\Commands\UnpublishEntry` (`entry.unpublish` version 1: the entry and the shared variant's version), with `Actions\PublishEntryAction` and `Actions\UnpublishEntryAction`, `#[Action]` on rest, inertia, mcp and cli. Both are composite and call no other write action: publish is `VariantReleasePlanner::plan()` followed by `PlacementPlanner::setWindow()` of the home placement (a placement of the entry below its home node) with its canonical moves, leaving out a release of the released revision and a window that changes nothing; unpublish is `VariantReleasePlanner::unrelease()` (`VariantUnreleased` of the released revision, or nothing) followed by `PlacementPlanner::close()` in each locale (`PlacementClosed`, a new contract mutation, for every placement `Visibility::visibleFrom()` shows now or later; withdrawn, hidden and expired ones stay). They read through `EntryReader` and `PlacementReader`, whose new `everyLocale()` gives every placement of the entry in every locale past the actor's regions through the owner function `cms_entry_placement_locales`. `PublishEntryAction` implements `Pipeline\RefusesCommand` (an unreachable home placement is `unauthorized`; another placement, a missing or withdrawn locale, and a missing or superfluous revision are `validation_failed`) and the new contract `Pipeline\ReportsVisibility`, which the `CommandPipeline` asks only on a dry run: `DryRunReport` now carries `visible`, a list of `Results\BecomesVisible` (placement, locale, time, sorted by placement and locale), computed by `Domain\VisibilityReport` from every placement before and after the plan, so a release lists the placements whose windows were open already. Unpublishing is decided on the home, so `Placements\Adapter\PlacementClosedWriter` closes through the owner function `cms_placement_close`, which runs only in the transaction of an `entry.unpublish` changeset by the context's actor (`placement.visibility_changed` to hidden), and `Entries\Adapter\VariantUnreleasedWriter` sets the head unreleased without a published revision, logs the `release_log` action `unreleased` (without a revision) and removes the type table's released row through `TypeRows::unrelease()`, keeping its values as the draft row where there is none (`variant.unreleased`, `Domain\Events\VariantUnreleased`, `VariantUnreleasedV1`); migration `*_add_publish_commands.php`. The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/PublishEntryTest.php` and `UnpublishEntryTest.php` (`packages/core/tests/Publishing/PublishingActionWorld`), and `packages/core/tests/Postgres/PublishingCommandsTest.php` and `PublishingSchemaTest.php` (`PublishingWorld`: one changeset each with the events of both sub-plans, placements closed outside the actor's regions, publishing again, stages none, and the same queries with 20 and 200 placements below the home node). `docs/addons/publish-commands.md` documents them.

## actor.deactivate

`actor.deactivate` (PRD 5.16, 6.4, invariant 37) is the command `Cbox\Cms\Core\Identity\Domain\Commands\DeactivateActor` (the actor and a `Contracts\Identity\DeactivationSource`, `local` by default or `inactivity`) with the write action `Identity\Actions\DeactivateActorAction`, exposed on no surface in M1. It reads the actor through the `ActorDirectory` and plans one `ActorDeactivated(actor, source)` for an active or pending actor, and nothing for one that is missing, deactivated or deprovisioned; the `CommandPipeline` rejects a plan without mutations with `validation_failed`, so a second deactivation changes nothing. The writer `Identity\Adapter\ActorDeactivatedWriter`, tagged under `MutationWriters::TAG`, calls the owner-role function `cms_identity_deactivate_actor(actor, version, source, changeset)` (migration `*_add_actor_deactivation.php`): one statement that sets the state `deactivated`, the context's version, the credential generation one higher and `actors.deactivation_source`, and ends every grant of the actor that has not ended by setting `grants.ended_changeset_id`, whatever the number of grants; it raises 42501 outside the transaction of an actor.deactivate changeset by the context's actor. It returns `actor.deactivated` (`Identity\Domain\Events\ActorDeactivated` with `ActorDeactivatedV1`: actor, source, credential generation and grants ended). An ended grant gives nothing and stays: `PostgresAccessResolver` and `cms_access_granted` skip it, and the unique key on actor, role and node is a partial unique index over grants that have not ended. The kernel keeps no compiled access regions, so nothing else is deleted. `tests/Postgres/WalkingSkeleton/DeactivatedActorTest.php` holds invariant 37: a command as or on behalf of a deactivated actor is `actor_not_active`, a command in flight fails with `version_conflict` when a deactivation in a child process commits while it waits on the actor's row lock, and a read with the actor's credential is `actor_not_active`. `packages/core/tests/Identity/DeactivationWorld.php` builds the real pipeline for it, also on a child process's own connection.

## actor.register and actor.activate

`actor.register` and `actor.activate` (PRD 5.16, 6.4, 12.2) are the commands `Cbox\Cms\Core\Identity\Domain\Commands\RegisterActor` (the caller's `ActorId`, the `ActorClass` staff or service, a `Contracts\Identity\DisplayName`, an `EmailAddress` and, for a service actor, the active staff actor responsible for it; it expects the actor absent) and `ActivateActor` (the actor and the `AggregateVersion` read), with `Identity\Actions\RegisterActorAction` on the CLI alone and `ActivateActorAction` on rest, inertia and cli; both implement `RefusesCommand` (`validation_failed` for an end user, a staff actor with a responsible person, a service actor without an active staff one, and an actor that is not pending). The plans are `ActorRegistered` (with the `ActorProfile`, personal, so it implements `Plans\ClassifiedMutation` and `HookPlans` shows a hook below personal access `withoutClassified()`) and `ActorActivated`; their writers call the owner functions `cms_identity_register_actor` and `cms_identity_activate_actor` (migration `*_add_actor_registration.php`, which also adds `actors.responsible_actor_id` and `actor_profiles` with forced row level security: an actor reads its own profile, and one whose grants hold a role permitted `actor.list` reads staff profiles at personal access, through `cms_access_profile_listed`), which run only in the transaction of a changeset of their command by the context's actor. The events `actor.registered` and `actor.activated` carry no text. Tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/RegisterActorTest.php` and `ActivateActorTest.php` (`Tests\Identity\ActorCommandFakes`) and `packages/core/tests/Postgres/ActorRegistrationTest.php` (`RegistrationWorld`); `docs/addons/actor-commands.md` documents them.

## grant.assign and grant.revoke

`grant.assign` and `grant.revoke` (PRD 5.10, 5.16, 6.4, invariant 31) live in the core module `Cbox\Cms\Core\Access`: `Domain\Commands\AssignGrant` (`grant.assign` version 1: the caller's `GrantId`, the `ActorId`, the `RoleId`, the `NodeId`, the `GrantEffect` and a list of `Locale`s or null for every locale; it expects the grant absent) and `Domain\Commands\RevokeGrant` (`grant.revoke` version 1: the grant and the `AggregateVersion` the caller read), with `Actions\AssignGrantAction` and `Actions\RevokeGrantAction`, `#[Action]` on rest, inertia and cli. They read through the port `Domain\GrantReader` (bound to `Adapter\PostgresGrantReader`; a fake and `GrantReaderBehaviour` in `packages/core/tests/Access`): a grant only where the context's regions reach its node, through the owner function `cms_access_grant`, so a grant outside them is `version_conflict`; a role with its permissions; and whether the grant's slot (`Domain\GrantSlotRef`, `grant_slot`, the actor, role and node, read as absent behind the commit's advisory lock) holds a grant that has not ended. An assign refuses with `validation_failed` an actor that is not an active staff or service actor, a role that does not exist, a locale named twice and a slot that is held; a revoke a grant that has ended. Their aggregates implement `Domain\GuardedGrant`, whose `escalation()` gives a `Domain\Dto\RoleGrant` for an allow (and for the end of a deny), and the kernel's `CommandAuthorizer`, after the command's own permission on the grant's node in its locales, holds it to the Domain class `Domain\EscalationGuard` for the actor and each actor of its chain, with every grant each holds and its role's permissions (`PostgresGrants::held()` and `heldByDelegator()`; `FakeCommandAuthorizer::granting()` does the same from `FakePermissions::held()`): every permission of the role reached on the node and on every node below it, which the node and the nodes of the issuer's grants strictly below it decide, in each of the grant's locales, and a classification access on each of them (the highest ceiling of the roles that reach it, capped by the credential's) not below the role's ceiling, else `grant_escalation_refused`; a role is administrative when one of its permissions is a command of the registry, a write (`PermissionCatalog::writes()`), in the `grant`, `role` or `identity` namespace or one of `actor.activate`, `actor.deactivate` and `actor.reactivate` (`Domain\AdministrativePermissions`, which the guard takes; queries such as `role.list` and `grant.list` never count), and a grant of one is refused with `step_up_required`, because step-up (PRD 5.16) is not built; `Pipeline\Domain\Dto\Authorization::refuse()` takes the code. The core tags the version locks `Adapter\PostgresGrantVersionLock` (`grant`), `PostgresRoleVersionLock` (`role`) and `PostgresGrantSlotLock` and the writers `GrantAssignedWriter` and `GrantRevokedWriter`, which call the owner functions `cms_access_assign_grant` and `cms_access_revoke_grant`, each only in the transaction of a changeset of its command by the context's actor (migration `*_add_grant_commands.php`), and return `grant.changed` (`Domain\Events\GrantChanged`, `GrantChangedV1`: actor, role, node). `GrantId` and `RoleId` implement `Identifier`. The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/EscalationGuardTest.php`, `GrantAssignTest.php` and `GrantRevokeTest.php` (`Tests\Access\GrantActionWorld`), the escalation cases of `CommandAuthorizerBehaviour`, and `packages/core/tests/Postgres/GrantCommandsTest.php` (`Tests\Access\GrantWorld`). The guard's grounds are read aggregates (invariant 31): an actor's set of grants is `Domain\ActorGrantsRef` (`actor_grants:<actor>`), whose version is one plus the versions of every grant of the actor, ended ones included, plus the number that have ended (`cms_access_actor_grants_versions`, migration `*_add_actor_grant_versions.php`); the authorizer reads the set of the actor and of each actor of its chain before their grants and returns them with an allowing guard decision (`Authorization::allow(...$reads)`, `relyingOn()`), the `CommandPipeline` adds them to the call's reads (one read twice at another version is `version_conflict`), grant.assign and grant.revoke read the set of the grant's actor and role.set_permissions the set of each holder (`GrantReader::actorGrants()`), and the commit locks every set through `Adapter\PostgresActorGrantsLock` (`cms_access_lock_actor_grants`: an exclusive transaction-scoped advisory lock per actor in id order, then the versions), so a revocation, a deny or a change of a held role that commits between the guard and the commit is `version_conflict`; `packages/core/tests/Postgres/GrantIssuerRaceTest.php` interleaves them on `IndependentConnections`. `docs/addons/grant-commands.md` documents them.

## role.create and role.set_permissions

`role.create` and `role.set_permissions` (PRD 5.10, 6.4, invariant 31) live in `Cbox\Cms\Core\Access` too: `Domain\Commands\CreateRole` (`role.create` version 1: the caller's `RoleId`, a `Contracts\Identity\RoleHandle`, the `ClassificationAccess` ceiling and the permissions as `CommandName`s; it expects the role absent) and `Domain\Commands\SetRolePermissions` (`role.set_permissions` version 1: the role, the `AggregateVersion` read and the full new list), with `Actions\CreateRoleAction` and `Actions\SetRolePermissionsAction`, `#[Action]` on rest, inertia and cli. A permission must be a name of the compiled registry, a command or a query, which the port `Domain\PermissionCatalog` says (bound to `Adapter\RegistryPermissionCatalog`; `FakePermissionCatalog` and `PermissionCatalogBehaviour` in `packages/core/tests/Access`), each once, or the action refuses it with `validation_failed` at `permissions[<n>]` (`Actions\RoleCommandRefusals`); a handle another role has is `validation_failed` (the aggregate `Domain\RoleHandleRef`, `role_handle`, read as absent behind the commit's advisory lock and locked by `Adapter\PostgresRoleHandleLock`), and a list equal to the role's plans nothing. `GrantReader` gained `roleGrants()`, every grant of a role that has not ended past the actor's regions through the owner function `cms_access_role_grants`, with the version of the role's set of grants (`Domain\RoleGrantsRef`, `role_grants`: one more than the grants ever given of the role, `cms_access_role_grants_version`, read after the role's lock by `Adapter\PostgresRoleGrantsLock`, so a grant.assign of the role meanwhile is `version_conflict`), and `handleTaken()`. The aggregates implement `Domain\GuardedRoleContent`, whose `roleContent()` gives a `Domain\Dto\RoleContentChange` (a new role's ceiling, the known permissions the change adds, the role's grants, and the permissions it held, from which the guard decides whether it becomes administrative), and the kernel's `CommandAuthorizer`, after the command's own permission anywhere, holds it to `EscalationGuard::ceiling()` against the context's classification access and `EscalationGuard::content()` for the actor and each actor of its chain: each added permission held on every node where an allow of the role has not ended and on every node below it, in that grant's locales, and for a change that takes a permission away role.set_permissions itself held on each of those nodes and every node below it, else `grant_escalation_refused`; a change that makes a granted role administrative is `step_up_required`. The plan of a change is `RolePermissionsSet` and a `GrantRoleContentChanged` per grant of the role, new contract mutations with `RoleCreated`; `Adapter\PostgresGrantVersionLock` is a `BatchVersionLock` (`cms_access_lock_grants`), and the writers `RoleCreatedWriter`, `RolePermissionsSetWriter` and the batch `GrantRoleContentChangedWriter` call the owner functions `cms_access_create_role`, `cms_access_set_role_permissions` and `cms_access_role_grants_changed`, each only in the transaction of a changeset of its command by the context's actor (migration `*_add_role_commands.php`); the last returns `grant.changed` for every grant. The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/RoleCommandsTest.php` (`GrantActionWorld`), the role content cases of `CommandAuthorizerBehaviour`, and `packages/core/tests/Postgres/RoleCommandsTest.php` (`GrantWorld`). `docs/addons/role-commands.md` documents them.

## site.register and cms:sites:sync

`site.register` and `cms:sites:sync` (PRD 5.8, 5.9, 11.14) live in the core module `Cbox\Cms\Core\Structure`: sites and hosts are configuration in git, and `cbox-cms.sites` gives each site, by handle, its `origin`, its `locales` (at least one, each once; `Routing\Boundary\SitesConfig` into `Routing\Domain\Dto\ConfiguredSite`) and other `hosts`. `cms:sites:sync` (the cli's `Console\SitesSyncCommand`, answered by `Cli\Boundary\SitesSyncOutput`) calls the action `Structure\Actions\SyncSites::sync(SitesSync)`, which reads each configured site by handle through the port `Structure\Domain\SiteDirectory` (bound to `Adapter\PostgresSiteDirectory`, through the owner lookups `cms_structure_site` and `cms_structure_site_named`, which need no actor context; a fake and `SiteDirectoryBehaviour` in `packages/core/tests/Structure`) and registers a site the database lacks through `RunMaintenanceCommand` as the installation operator, with the unit of work `sites:<handle>:<sha256 of the sorted locale tags>`; a site registered with the same locales is left alone, and one with other locales is `site_locales_drift` (exit 65) and nothing of it is written, while the other sites are still synced (`Domain\Dto\SitesSyncReport` of `SiteSync`s with a `Domain\SiteSyncOutcome`). The command is `Domain\Commands\RegisterSite` (`site.register` version 1: the caller's `SiteId`, the `Routing\Domain\SiteHandle`, the caller's root `NodeId` and the `Locale`s; it expects the site and the aggregate `Domain\SiteHandleRef` (`site_handle`, read as absent behind the commit's advisory lock, `Adapter\PostgresSiteHandleLock`) absent) with `Actions\RegisterSiteAction`, exposed on no surface and named in `MaintenanceAuthorizer::COMMANDS`; it plans one `Plans\Mutations\SiteRegistered`, which `Adapter\SiteRegisteredWriter` writes through the owner function `cms_structure_register_site` (migration `*_add_site_registration.php`; only in the transaction of a site.register changeset by the context's actor): the root node of kind site, the site, its `site_locales` and the route `/` in each locale, returning `site.registered` (`Domain\Events\SiteRegistered`, `SiteRegisteredV1`: site, root, locales). The workbench configures the site `workbench` in da and en, and `composer dev:prepare` ends with `cms:sites:sync`. The tests: `packages/core/tests/Actions/RegisterSiteTest.php` and `SyncSitesTest.php` (`Tests\Structure\SiteCommandFakes`), `packages/core/tests/Postgres/SiteRegistrationTest.php` and `packages/cli/tests/Postgres/SitesSyncCommandTest.php`. `docs/addons/site-commands.md` documents them.

## The access queries

The access queries (PRD 5.8, 5.10, 5.16, 12.2) are `role.list`, `grant.list`, `actor.list` and `node.list`, each version 1 on rest and inertia, with a schema pair in `packages/core/resources/schemas/queries` and generated codecs: `Access\Domain\Queries\ListRoles` and `ListGrants` with `Access\Actions\ListRolesAction` and `ListGrantsAction` (`Access\Domain\Dto\RoleList`, `ListedRole`, `GrantList`, `ListedGrant`; `Access\Domain\RoleHandle`), `Identity\Domain\Queries\ListActors` with `Identity\Actions\ListActorsAction` (`ActorList`, `ListedActor`, and `Identity\Domain\Dto\ListedProfile`, the display name and email, classified personal), and `Structure\Domain\Queries\ListNodes` with `Structure\Actions\ListNodesAction` (`Structure\Domain\Dto\NodeList`, `ListedNode`). Each reads a page: `after` (an id, null from the start) and `limit` (1 to `Reads\Domain\ListLimit::MAX`, 100, default 50); the action asks its port for one row more and answers `next`, and costs the limit. role.list, grant.list and actor.list need a role whose permissions name them; node.list implements the contract marker `Contracts\Pipeline\ActorQuery`, which `PostgresQueryAuthorizer` and `FakeQueryAuthorizer::granting()` allow for every actor principal and refuse to the anonymous one. The ports are `Access\Domain\AccessListings` (`Adapter\PostgresAccessListings`: the roles and their permissions as the app role, the grants through the owner function `cms_access_grant_list`), `Identity\Domain\ActorListing` (`Adapter\PostgresActorListing`, through `cms_identity_actor_list`) and `Structure\Domain\NodeListing` (`Structure\Adapter\PostgresNodeListing`, through `cms_access_node_list`), from the migration `*_add_access_listings.php` with `cms_access_holds_permission` and `cms_access_node_label` (the path label: the site's handle for the root of a site's tree, then each node's last route segment or its kind, joined by `/`). The functions give nothing without an actor context; grant.list and actor.list give nothing to an actor that does not hold their permission; a grant only on a node the context reaches and where a role of the actor whose permissions name grant.list reaches it by the nearest-grant rule (`cms_access_permits`, migration `*_list_grants_where_grant_list_reaches.php`; `packages/core/tests/Postgres/GrantListPermissionTest.php`); a profile only for the context's own actor or, at personal access, for any other (actor.list through `cms_access_profile_listed`), else null; and the codec writes a profile's values only at personal access, so below it a profile is `{}`. Fakes `FakeAccessListings`, `FakeActorListing` and `FakeNodeListing` in `packages/core/tests/<Feature>/Fakes`, held to the Postgres adapters by `AccessListingsBehaviour`, `ActorListingBehaviour` and `NodeListingBehaviour`; the action tests are `packages/core/tests/Actions/RoleListTest.php`, `GrantListTest.php`, `ActorListTest.php` and `NodeListTest.php` (`Tests\Access\ListingWorld`), and `packages/http/tests/Rest/AccessQueriesParityTest.php` holds REST and the Inertia props to the same documents. The page is `docs/addons/access-queries.md`.
