## Do and don't

- Do give it the type's descriptor as `type.describe` answers it, and the reader's locale; the labels, instructions and choices are in that locale, or the blueprint's own text.
- Do start a new entry with `newEntryFields(descriptor, now)`, so its defaults, today and now are in the fields.
- Do set `showErrors="all"` once the editor tries to save, and give the server's errors back by path; they are shown before the client's own.
- Do replace an input through `inputs` by its base, such as a rich text editor, behind the `EntryFieldInput` contract.
- Don't validate the fields with a validator of your own: `validateEntry()` is the generated validation runtime over the descriptor's rules, the same the kernel holds to its InputValidator.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
