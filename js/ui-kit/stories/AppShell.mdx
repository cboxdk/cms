## Do and don't

- Do render one shell per page, around a Page.
- Do put the command palette's button and the account in the actions.
- Don't put a page's own actions in the shell's top bar; they belong in its PageHeader.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
