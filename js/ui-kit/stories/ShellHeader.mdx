## Do and don't

- Do put the installation's Brand at the start and the shell's few actions, such as signing out, at the end.
- Don't put navigation or page actions in the shell's header; they belong in the side navigation and the PageHeader.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
