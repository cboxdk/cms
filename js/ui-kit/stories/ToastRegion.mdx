## Do and don't

- Do render one region per page, and add a toast for something that happened elsewhere, such as a saved change.
- Do keep a toast that asks the reader to act until it is closed.
- Don't put the only copy of an error in a toast; show it where it happened.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
