## Do and don't

- Do give the row's own title, and say what the row is in the detail, such as its type.
- Do mark the caller's text for a row without a title as a fallback.
- Don't put an action in it; a row's actions belong in its menu.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
