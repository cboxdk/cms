## Do and don't

- Do say what happened and what to do next, with an action when there is one.
- Do use danger for a refusal and success for a saved change.
- Don't use a callout for an error at one field; the field shows it, and an ErrorSummary lists them.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
