## Do and don't

- Do give every page and command the reader may open an entry, with keywords such as the command's name.
- Do render one palette per page, in the AppShell.
- Don't list a command the reader may not run; the server leaves it out.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
