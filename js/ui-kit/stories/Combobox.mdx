## Do and don't

- Do let the combobox filter a short list itself, and filter a long one on the server through onInputChange.
- Do say what the list waits for while options load, and what failed when they do not.
- Don't use a combobox for a few options the reader should see at once; use a RadioGroup or a Select.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
