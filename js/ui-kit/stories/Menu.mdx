## Do and don't

- Do name the trigger after what the actions act on, such as "Actions for Ada".
- Do mark an action that removes or revokes with the danger tone.
- Don't put navigation in a menu; use SideNav or links.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
