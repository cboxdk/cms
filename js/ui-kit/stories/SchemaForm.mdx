## Do and don't

- Do read the schema with `readCommandSchema()` once and keep the model; it throws `SchemaUnsupported` for a keyword the form has no field for, which the page shows instead of a form.
- Do validate the document with the command's generated validator before you submit, and give the errors back by path, so each is shown at its field.
- Do pass the catalogue's texts for the labels and descriptions; the schema's title and description are the fallback.
- Don't use it where a page has a form of its own with pickers for its values; it is the form for any command.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
