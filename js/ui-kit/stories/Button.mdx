## Do and don't

- Do give the one main action of a form or a page the primary variant.
- Do say what the button does in its text, such as "Assign a grant".
- Don't use a button to go to another page; use a TextLink.
- Don't put two primary buttons side by side.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
