## Do and don't

- Do ask before an action that cannot simply be undone, such as revoking a grant.
- Do name the confirming button after the action, such as "Revoke".
- Don't ask before an action that can be undone in a click; it trains readers to confirm without reading.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
