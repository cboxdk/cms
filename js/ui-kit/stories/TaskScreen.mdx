# TaskScreen

Stability: **experimental**. Since: **1.0**.

<Description of={TaskScreenStories} />

<Canvas of={TaskScreenStories.SignIn} />

With a showcase, the side that says what the product is sits beside the task on a wide screen and below it on a phone, on the cool canvas with a card, as the sign-in pages of the panel show it.

<Canvas of={TaskScreenStories.WithShowcase} />

## Do and don't

- Do use it for one task outside the panel's navigation, such as signing in.
- Don't put the panel's navigation on it.
- Do keep the showcase to what the product does; don't show numbers the page has not measured.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
