## Do and don't

- Do show the report of a dry run before the reader saves the change.
- Do pass the time zone the reader works in.
- Don't show it for a committed change; use a ReceiptStatus.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
