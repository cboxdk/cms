## Do and don't

- Do use it for a setting of how something is shown, such as a list's density, with two to five short choices.
- Do keep one choice made; the control never lets the last one go.
- Don't use it to switch between views of different things; use Tabs.

## Accessibility

The keyboard and screen reader behaviour is described above and held by the stories' play functions, which run as tests in gate 7 with axe; every story is shown in the dark theme, in forced colours and in Danish.
